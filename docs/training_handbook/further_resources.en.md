# Which other teaching materials are available?

If you need additional teaching materials to help you prepare and conduct your RDM training in agricultural science, you can find additional resources here.

*[RDM]: Research data management


## List of all FAIRagro OERs

You can find an overview of all training materials published by FAIRagro [here](https://zenodo.org/communities/nfdi-fairagro/records?q=&f=subject%3AOER&f=subject%3AOpen%20Educational%20Resources&f=resource_type%3Alesson&f=resource_type%3Apresentation&f=resource_type%3Apublication&l=list&p=1&s=10&sort=newest){:target="_blank"}.

## Discipline-Specific RDM materials

Our [collection of learning and teaching materials](https://fairagro.net/wo-finde-ich-lern-und-lehrmaterialien-zum-thema-fdm/){:target=“_blank”} includes numerous other resources from the field of agricultural sciences and related research areas that can be reused for teaching purposes.

## Generic and interdisciplinary RDM materials

- [DALIA](https://search.dalia.education/basic/){:target=“_blank”} allows you to search for Open Educational Resources (OER) related to FDM as well as those in the context of the NFDI. You can also filter by discipline here, e.g., [Agricultural, Forest, and Nutritional Sciences; Veterinary Medicine](https://search.dalia.education/search/?query=&offset=0&source=basic&http%3A%2F%2Fpurl.org%2Fspar%2Ffabio%2FDiscipline=https%3A%2F%2Fw3id.org%2Fkim%2Fhochschulfaechersystematik%2Fn7){:target="_blank"}.
- The [resource collection](https://rs.cms.hu-berlin.de/uag_fdm/pages/home.php?login=true){:target="_blank"} of [DINI/Nestor UAG Schulung und Fortbildung](https://www.forschungsdaten.org/index.php/UAG_Schulungen/Fortbildungen){:target="_blank"} (engl.: Training and Continuing Education) allows you to search for teaching materials on RDM.


## Didactic Framework

- German-language [FDM Train-the-Trainer Concept](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1215376){:target="_blank"} from [DINI/Nestor UAG Schulung und Fortbildung](https://www.forschungsdaten.org/index.php/UAG_Schulungen/Fortbildungen){:target="_blank"}  (engl.: Training and Continuing Education) covers comprehensive, generic aspects of research data management as well as didactic principles for developing teaching and workshop concepts.
- The [RDM Learning Objective Matrix](https://www.forschungsdaten.org/index.php/Lernzielmatrix){:target="_blank"} summarizes relevant educational content and associated learning objectives at the specified qualification levels in a standardized format.
